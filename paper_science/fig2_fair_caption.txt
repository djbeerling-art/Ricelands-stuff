Fig. 2. Climate response to riceland enhanced weathering. Change in effective radiative forcing (A, B) and global mean surface temperature (C, D) from avoided CH4 (blue), avoided N2O (orange), CO2 removal net of transport emissions (green) and all combined (black), for deployment on 95.8 Mha of rice within 150 km of basalt at 20 t ha-1 yr-1. (A, C) Deployment 2026-2050, then stopped. (B, D) Year-25 rates held to 2100 (an assumption beyond the simulated period). FaIR 2.2.5 with the 841-member constrained calibration (v1.4.1) against an SSP2-4.5 background; lines are ensemble medians, bands 5-95%. Shading marks the deployment period. Present-climate soil simulations.
